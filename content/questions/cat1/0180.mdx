dBc = dB relative to the carrier. This is used e. g. to specify the power of a sideband in a modulated signal relative to the carier. For example, -30 dBc means that the sideband is 30 dB below the carier, 1.e., it has a 1000 times lower power. dBc/Hz: this is used for noise and means dBc in a 1-Hz bandwidth. A136
